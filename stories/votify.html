Votify

Explore political promises and follow them back to source documents.

My part · Backend developmentOctober 2025 · HackHarvard
ContextMy contributionInside the work

Can a reader look past a claim and inspect its evidence?

Votify puts political commitments into an explorable interface. A reader can browse the collection, open a detail view, and follow the linked sources behind a commitment.

My contribution

I worked on the backend workflow coordinating Gemini, Perplexity, and Llama to cross-check commitments against source documents.

Team: Peter Yuk — backend; Blessing Amoah — prompt engineering; Araf Latif and Dylan D’rozario — frontend.

Open a claim. Follow the source.

01 · Start with the collection.

The dashboard groups political figures and commitments, with filters for narrowing the view.

The screen gives a reader a way into the collection without opening every source document first.

02 · Open the reasoning.

The detail view brings commitments, model assessments, and supporting evidence into the same place.

The screenshot includes a model-disagreement message. The interface exposes that uncertainty alongside its assessment.

03 · Continue to the documents.

Evidence entries include links to supporting documents so a reader can inspect the source.

A source link makes the output inspectable. Linking coverage alone does not establish that an assessment is correct.
